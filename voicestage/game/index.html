YOUR FIRST ENCOUNTER

A way through the gate.

Toren guards the only passage into Ashenfort. What you do matters as much as what you say.

  1. 01
    Start a conversation

    Ask to enter, introduce yourself, or ask about the fortress. Toren remembers recent turns.

  2. 02
    Make a choice

    Help recover his supplies to earn passage. Steal his key and the gate closes—even if you helped.

  3. 03
    Hear the difference

    Open Voice Studio to audition three profiles in English and Hindi. Action buttons trigger spoken reactions. Stop a line or replay audio already received.

Conversation cannot change the game state. Use the action buttons to help Toren or take and return the key.

Connect Sarvam

The downloadable FastAPI project includes this scene and the full integration. Set your key in the server’s .env file, then restart:

SARVAM_API_KEY=your_key_here
uvicorn app:app --reload

For this hosted version, set SARVAM_API_KEY as a secret in the Site’s runtime environment and redeploy. The key is never entered into the game or sent to the browser.
